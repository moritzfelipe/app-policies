Privacy policy: YouTube connector

Last updated: 2026-10-07

"YouTube connector" is a personal, single-user Google API client. It lets the owner upload and schedule videos to their own YouTube channels and read those channels' statistics and analytics. It uses the YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

What Google data it accesses

  • youtube.readonly and youtube: the owner's channel and video details, and changing the visibility or publish time of the owner's own videos.
  • youtube.upload: uploading videos to the owner's channel.
  • yt-analytics.readonly and yt-analytics-monetary.readonly: the owner's channel analytics (views, watch time, retention, traffic sources, demographics, revenue).

How the data is used

  • The data is used solely to publish the owner's own videos and to see how they perform.
  • Access tokens and any data read are stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • No Google user data is collected, stored, or processed by the developer on any other server.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold. It is not shared with any third party, except that the owner may use AI assistants on their own computer to analyse their own channel's numbers.

AI and machine learning

Google user data is not used to develop, improve, or train any AI or machine-learning model.

Compliance

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or via the Google security settings page.
