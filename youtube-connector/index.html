YouTube connector

A personal, single-user Google API client. The owner uses it on their own computer to upload and schedule videos to their own YouTube channels and to read those channels' statistics and analytics.

It is not a public service. It is used by one person, for their own channels only.

  • Requested access: youtube.readonly, youtube.upload, youtube, yt-analytics.readonly, yt-analytics-monetary.readonly.
  • Uses the YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.
