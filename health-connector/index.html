Health connector

A personal, single-user Google API client. The owner uses it on their own computer to read their own Fitbit / Google Health data (activity, sleep, heart and body measurements, nutrition) into a private dashboard, and to log their own entries such as meals, workouts, and measurements.

It is not a public service. It is used by one person, for their own account only.

  • Requested access (Google Health API): googlehealth.activity_and_fitness.readonly, googlehealth.activity_and_fitness.writeonly, googlehealth.sleep.readonly, googlehealth.health_metrics_and_measurements.readonly, googlehealth.health_metrics_and_measurements.writeonly, googlehealth.nutrition.readonly, googlehealth.nutrition.writeonly, googlehealth.ecg.readonly, googlehealth.irn.readonly, googlehealth.location.readonly, googlehealth.profile.readonly, googlehealth.settings.readonly.
  • All data stays on the owner's own computer.
