Privacy policy: Health connector

Last updated: 2026-10-12

"Health connector" is a personal, single-user Google API client. It lets the owner read their own Fitbit / Google Health data into a private dashboard on their own computer, and log their own entries. It uses the Google Health API; see the Google Privacy Policy.

What Google data it accesses

  • activity_and_fitness (read and write): steps, active zone minutes, and exercise sessions; adding the owner's own workouts.
  • sleep (read): sleep sessions and sleep stages.
  • health_metrics_and_measurements (read and write): heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, and body measurements such as weight; adding the owner's own measurements.
  • nutrition (read and write): food and nutrition logs; adding the owner's own meals.
  • ecg and irn (read): ECG readings and irregular rhythm notifications.
  • location (read): GPS routes recorded during the owner's exercise.
  • profile and settings (read): profile details (such as height and age) and account settings (such as units).

How the data is used

  • The data is used solely to show the owner their own health data and scores, and to log their own entries.
  • Access tokens and all data read are stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • No Google user data is collected, stored, or processed by the developer on any other server.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold. It is not shared with any third party, except that the owner may use AI assistants on their own computer to analyse their own health data.

AI and machine learning

Google user data is not used to develop, improve, or train any AI or machine-learning model.

Compliance

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local token file and database on the owner's computer removes all stored data.
